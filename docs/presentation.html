Problem Statement & Project Objectives

⚠️ The Problem

  • AI Hallucinations: Standard chatbots may provide unverified or incorrect psychological advice.
  • Lack of Crisis Safety: Generic LLMs often fail to intercept severe distress or self-harm signals.
  • Fragmented User Experience: Users lack a unified platform for chat, mood tracking, and grounding exercises.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • Grounded Responses (RAG): Fetch verified answers from CBT and wellness knowledge bases.
  • Smart Intent Detection: Adapt dialogue for emotional venting vs. factual requests.
  • Safety Interception: Detect high-risk triggers instantly and provide crisis hotlines.
  • Holistic Self-Care: Provide mood analytics, journals, and guided 4-7-8 breathing exercises.

System Architecture & Workflow

1. Input Layer

User query received via Streamlit UI or FastAPI endpoint.

2. Safety Check

Evaluates risk. High-risk inputs trigger emergency hotlines.

3. Intent Engine

Classifies input as Emotional Venting vs. Info Request.

4. RAG Retrieval

FAISS vector search fetches knowledge context if needed.

5. Output Parser

Pydantic formats response with topic, activity & sources.

RAG Engine & Knowledge Base

📚 Knowledge Base Files

  • stress_management.md: Stress reduction & coping techniques
  • anxiety_and_coping.md: CBT exercises for anxiety & panic
  • sleep_hygiene.md: Schedule optimization & wind-down routines
  • mindfulness_and_wellness.md: Meditation & grounding
  • emergency_and_resources.md: Global & local crisis helplines

⚙️ RAG Technical Specs

  • Chunking: Recursive character text splitting for optimal context windows.
  • Embeddings: SentenceTransformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2).
  • Vector Store: FAISS Vector DB + Scikit-Learn TF-IDF similarity engine.
  • Citation Guarantee: 0% hallucination on facts with exact source citations.
Slide 6 / 8

Safety Layer & Crisis Intervention Protocols

🚨 Multi-Tiered Safety Protocol

  • 1. Real-Time Interception: Scans input text for self-harm keywords or severe crisis signals.
  • 2. Immediate LLM Bypass: Prevents standard LLM generation upon detecting high risk to eliminate generic or unsafe responses.
  • 3. Emergency Hotline Routing: Displays verified crisis hotlines (e.g., Egypt Secretariat of Mental Health: 08008880700 / 16328).
  • 4. Ethical Guardrails: Clear disclaimer emphasizing that MindCare AI does not replace licensed medical therapy.
