# Stress Management & Educational Guide

## Overview of Stress
Stress is a natural physiological and psychological reaction to life experiences. Everyone expresses stress from time to time. Anything from daily responsibilities like work and family to serious life events can trigger stress.

## Common Signs and Symptoms
- **Physical**: Tension headaches, muscle tightness, fatigue, elevated heart rate, sleep disturbances.
- **Emotional**: Overwhelmed feelings, irritability, anxiety, restlessness, mood swings.
- **Cognitive**: Difficulty concentrating, racing thoughts, constant worrying, forgetfulness.
- **Behavioral**: Changes in appetite, procrastinating or neglecting responsibilities, withdrawal from social activities.

## Evidence-Based Coping Strategies
1. **Time Management & Prioritization**: Break down large tasks into smaller, manageable steps. Use techniques like the Pomodoro method or Eisenhower Matrix.
2. **Deep Breathing & Relaxation**: Practice diaphragmatic breathing (e.g., 4-7-8 technique) to activate the parasympathetic nervous system and lower cortisol levels.
3. **Physical Activity**: Regular exercise releases endorphins, improves sleep quality, and decreases body tension.
4. **Cognitive Reframing**: Identify automatic negative thoughts (ANTs) and replace them with realistic, balanced perspectives.
5. **Setting Healthy Boundaries**: Learn to say no to non-essential commitments when your capacity is full.

## When to Seek Professional Support
If stress becomes chronic, overwhelming, or interferes with daily functioning (such as work, school, or relationships), consulting a mental health professional (such as a psychologist or counselor) is recommended.
