# Emergency Support & Crisis Resources

## Safety Disclaimer
MindCare AI is an educational and supportive AI companion. It does **NOT** provide medical diagnosis, clinical therapy, or emergency crisis intervention. 

## Emergency Helplines & Crisis Contacts

If you or someone you know is experiencing a mental health emergency, severe distress, or thoughts of self-harm, please reach out immediately to emergency services or professional helplines:

### International Crisis Resources
- **International Association for Suicide Prevention (IASP)**: https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/
- **Befrienders Worldwide**: https://www.befrienders.org/

### Regional Helplines
- **United States & Canada**:
  - Emergency Call: **911**
  - Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: **988** (Call or Text, 24/7)
  - Crisis Text Line: Text **HOME** to **741741**
- **United Kingdom**:
  - Emergency Call: **999**
  - Samaritans Hotline: **116 123**
- **Egypt & Middle East**:
  - General Emergency: **123**
  - General Secretariat of Mental Health (Egypt): **08008880700** / **0220816831**
- **Europe**:
  - Emergency Call: **112**

## What to Do in Acute Crisis
1. Connect immediately with a crisis hotline or local emergency services.
2. Reach out to a trusted family member, friend, or mental health professional.
3. Move to a safe environment away from potential harm.
