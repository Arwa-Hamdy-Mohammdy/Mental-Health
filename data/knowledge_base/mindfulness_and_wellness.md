# Mindfulness & Emotional Wellbeing Guide

## Introduction to Mindfulness
Mindfulness is the practice of purposefully paying attention to the present moment with openness, curiosity, and without judgement. It allows individuals to observe thoughts and feelings without becoming overwhelmed by them.

## Daily Mindfulness Exercises
- **Mindful Breathing**: Spend 3-5 minutes focusing solely on the sensation of air entering and leaving your nostrils or the rising and falling of your abdomen.
- **Body Scan Meditation**: Slowly shift attention through different parts of your body from toes to head, noticing sensations without trying to change them.
- **Gratitude Journaling**: Write down three specific things you are grateful for each day to foster positive cognitive framing.

## Emotional Regulation Strategies
1. **Name the Emotion**: Simply identifying "I am feeling anxious" or "I am feeling overwhelmed" reduces amygdala activation.
2. **Self-Compassion**: Treat yourself with the same kindness and understanding you would offer a good friend experiencing a tough time.
3. **Pacing**: Recognize when your energy is low and allow yourself rest without feeling guilty.
