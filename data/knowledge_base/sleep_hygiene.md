# Sleep Hygiene & Relaxation Resources

## The Importance of Sleep for Mental Health
Sleep and mental health are closely connected. Quality sleep promotes emotional resilience, cognitive clarity, and mood regulation. Poor sleep can exacerbate stress, anxiety, and depressive symptoms.

## Healthy Sleep Practices (Sleep Hygiene)
1. **Maintain a Consistent Schedule**: Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends.
2. **Optimize Sleep Environment**: Keep your bedroom cool, dark, and quiet. Consider using blackout curtains or a white noise machine if needed.
3. **Screen-Free Wind-Down**: Discontinue using electronic devices (phones, laptops, TVs) at least 30 to 60 minutes before bedtime to prevent blue light from suppressing melatonin.
4. **Avoid Stimulants**: Refrain from consuming caffeine, heavy meals, or intense workouts close to bedtime.
5. **Associate Bed with Sleep**: If you cannot fall asleep after 20 minutes, get out of bed and do a quiet, relaxing activity (like reading) until you feel sleepy.

## Evening Relaxation Routine
- Take a warm bath or shower.
- Practice gentle stretching or restorative yoga.
- Listen to calming ambient music or guided sleep meditation.
- Drink a warm, non-caffeinated herbal tea (such as chamomile).
